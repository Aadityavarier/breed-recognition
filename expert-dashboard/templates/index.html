The Problem

India's livestock data has a quality crisis

The Bharat Pashudhan App registers over 300 million cattle and buffalo across India. But manual breed entry by field workers — under time pressure, without reference standards — produces systematic misregistration that flows through the entire policy chain.

❌

Wrong breed → Wrong subsidy

PM-KISAN, Rashtriya Gokul Mission, and state dairy schemes disburse benefits based on registered breed. A misidentified Sahiwal registered as a crossbreed disqualifies the farmer from indigenous cattle incentives worth ₹10,000–₹50,000 per animal.

📋

Manual records enable fraud

Without a photo-verified, tamper-evident audit trail, phantom animal registrations and duplicate claims are difficult to detect. Field-level data entry without any AI cross-check is a known vulnerability in livestock insurance programs.

🧬

Indigenous genetics are being lost

Breeds like Vechur, Punganur, and Alambadi number in the thousands — not millions. Without accurate breed-level census data, conservation and cross-breeding programs cannot be targeted to the right districts and herds.

📡

AI must work where internet doesn't

75% of India's livestock is registered in rural areas with intermittent or zero connectivity. Any AI solution that requires a cloud API call at the point of identification is not deployable at scale across India.

The Solution

From photo to verified record — entirely offline

A four-step pipeline that runs on a low-cost Android tablet, no internet required.

1
📸

FLW takes a photo

Field-level worker captures a standard-angle photo of the animal using the BPA camera within the app. No specialist training required.

2
🤖

On-device AI identifies breed

A quantised MobileNetV2 model runs entirely on-device in under 500ms. Region and coat colour metadata further refine the prediction with a heuristic fusion layer.

3
✅

Auto-verify or expert review

High-confidence results are auto-accepted. Low-confidence or rare-breed scans are routed to a veterinarian expert queue, with a Grad-CAM explainability overlay.

4
☁️

Synced to BPA on reconnect

Verified records — each with a SHA-256 hash chain, QR passport, and Pashu Aadhaar ID — are batch-synced to Bharat Pashudhan when connectivity returns.

Who It's For

Built for every link in India's livestock chain

Three different stakeholders, one integrated platform — each with a dedicated workflow.

📱

Field Level Workers

FLWs & Paravets

The frontline of India's livestock data system — working across hundreds of villages daily with basic smartphones and unreliable connectivity.

  • Identify any of 15 indigenous breeds instantly — no manual lookup or memory required
  • Works fully offline — 2G, 3G, or no signal at all
  • Hindi and regional language UI toggle
  • Auto-generates a Pashu Aadhaar ID and QR code for each animal
🩺

Veterinarians & Experts

Expert Review Panel

Specialists who validate borderline predictions and maintain the scientific integrity of the breed census data.

  • Dedicated Expert Queue tab — only receives low-confidence scans that need review
  • Grad-CAM XAI overlay shows exactly which features the AI used — accountable, explainable decisions
  • One-click Verify or Flag for Retraining actions
  • Full SHA-256 audit trail — every decision is permanently logged and tamper-evident
🌾

Farmers & Dairy Breeders

Livestock Owners

The ultimate beneficiaries — small and marginal farmers whose livelihoods depend on correct breed registration.

  • Correct breed registration ensures access to the right government subsidy schemes
  • Accurate livestock insurance coverage based on verified breed value
  • Science-based crossbreeding advisory from the Encyclopedia to optimise milk yield
  • A tamper-proof QR-coded identity for each animal — reducing theft and fraud
Public Impact

Why accurate breed data is a national priority

India has the world's largest livestock population. The quality of this data directly affects ₹15,000+ crore in annual government expenditure on animal husbandry schemes.

📊

Better Livestock Census

Accurate breed-level data improves the 20th Livestock Census, enabling evidence-based national breeding policy and district-level resource allocation.

💰

Fair Subsidy Disbursal

Breed-correct registration ensures Rashtriya Gokul Mission incentives and state dairy bonuses actually reach the farmers who own high-value indigenous cattle.

🧬

Indigenous Genetics Preserved

Knowing exactly where rare breeds like Vechur or Punganur exist allows targeted conservation programs and regulated semen supply — preventing irreversible genetic erosion.

🛡️

Reduced Livestock Fraud

Photo-verified, hash-chained records make duplicate insurance claims and phantom animal registrations detectable — protecting the integrity of the entire BPA dataset.

Breed Spotlight

15 Curated Indigenous Breeds

Every breed in this platform was selected based on ICAR breed registers, conservation status, and economic significance to Indian dairy and agriculture.

🐄
Gir
Milch · Gujarat
Highest milk yield among Indian zebu cattle; exported to Brazil and Israel for tropical dairy programs.
🐄
Sahiwal
Milch · Punjab
India's premier dairy zebu; tick-resistant and heat-tolerant — the backbone of Punjab and Haryana dairy economies.
🐃
Murrah
Buffalo · Haryana
World's highest-yielding buffalo breed; produces milk with 7%+ fat, critical for Indian ghee and paneer industries.
🐄
Kankrej
Dual Purpose · Gujarat
Hardy dual-purpose breed; excellent draught power plus moderate milk yield; parent of the American Brahman.
🐄
Khillari
Draught · Maharashtra
Renowned for speed and endurance; preferred bullocks for the Deccan plateau's hard, rocky soils.
🐄
Hariana
Dual Purpose · Haryana
Versatile dual-purpose breed widely used in UP and Haryana; significant in government cattle development programs.
🐄
Kangayam
Draught · Tamil Nadu
Critically endangered; one of the last pure draught breeds of South India with exceptional disease resistance.
🐄
Vechur
Milch · Kerala
World's smallest cattle breed; high A2 milk fat; near-extinct and under active conservation by Kerala Veterinary University.
🐄
Red Sindhi
Milch · Sindh origin
High heat and tick tolerance; a key breed in India's tropical dairy belt from Gujarat to West Bengal.
🐄
Ongole
Dual Purpose · Andhra Pradesh
Iconic draught breed of coastal Andhra; exported globally as Nellore cattle to Brazil and the USA.

Bharat Pashudhan AI Portal

India is home to 43 recognised cattle breeds and 13 buffalo breeds — the highest diversity of any country. This platform supports the accurate identification and conservation of these breeds, which are uniquely adapted to India's diverse agro-climatic zones and are irreplaceable genetic resources for drought tolerance, disease resistance, and high-fat A2 milk production.
