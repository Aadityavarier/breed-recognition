Digital Livestock Intelligence Architecture

How offline AI at the point of data entry transforms national livestock management.

Our Mission

Fixing Breed Data at Point of Entry

Manual breed registration often leads to errors affecting subsidy allocation and insurance claims. Bharat Pashu-Pehchaan provides instant, objective AI validation right when data is recorded in the field.

How It Works

Photo to Verified Audit Record

Field Level Workers capture a single photo. Quantized MobileNetV2 identifies morphological features offline, outputs confidence scores, flags ambiguous cases for expert review, and generates SHA-256 audit hashes.

Field Capability

Built for Zero-Connectivity Field Use

Designed for low-cost Android smartphones and tablets in remote rural areas. Runs 100% offline with IndexedDB local caching, Web Speech audio readout for accessibility, and automatic cloud sync when online.

Bharat Pashu-Pehchaan

India is home to 43 recognised cattle breeds and 13 buffalo breeds — the highest diversity of any country. This platform supports the accurate identification and conservation of these breeds, which are uniquely adapted to India's diverse agro-climatic zones and are irreplaceable genetic resources for drought tolerance, disease resistance, and high-fat A2 milk production.
